Arm a signal/slot connection that fires exactly once.

Qt 6 has Qt::SingleShotConnection; Qt 5.15 does not, so under Qt 5 the connection must be torn down by the slot on first entry (see disconnectSingleShot()). Any stale connection to the same slot is disconnected first so repeated arming never accumulates connections.

Tear down a connectSingleShot() connection from inside its slot.

A no-op on Qt 6 (Qt::SingleShotConnection already self-disconnects). On Qt 5.15 it disconnects the slot on first entry so it cannot fire again. Call it once at the top of a slot armed with connectSingleShot().
